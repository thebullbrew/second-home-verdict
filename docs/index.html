The Second Home Verdict

Everyone quotes the purchase price. Nobody quotes the price per night they actually sleep there. This does — owning vs renting the same weeks and investing the difference, on the same cash.

Start with a place

The house

How you'll actually use it

Honest rule: principal you pay down is not a cost — it comes back in equity. Interest, tax, insurance, HOA, upkeep and travel are. Renting gets credit for investing the down payment instead of parking it in a house you visit five weeks a year.

The method

Own: simulate the mortgage month by month for the hold. Add every carrying dollar — tax, insurance, HOA, maintenance, empty-house utilities, and your travel to get there. Subtract STR net income if you rent it out. At exit, sale price after appreciation, minus selling costs, minus the remaining loan, is your equity back. Economic cost = cash in − equity back.

Rent: pay the comparable nightly rate for exactly the nights you'd use, inflated yearly. Invest the down payment + closing cash at your return instead. Economic cost = rent paid − investment gains on that cash.

LIFESTYLE TAX fires when your true cost per night is more than double the rental rate — at that point you're not buying lodging, you're buying a feeling. Price the feeling honestly and decide if it's worth it.
